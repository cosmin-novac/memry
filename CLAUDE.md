# Writing rules for this repository

These apply to replies, commit messages, docstrings, comments and docs.

- Never give objects agency. Only people act. A question does not "name" anyone; a person asks about someone. A search does not "find"; Memry returns, or the person gets. A memory does not "hold" or "say"; it contains, it is. A number does not "tell". Before writing a sentence, ask who or what does the verb. A thing with a human verb is rewritten with the person, or with "is", "has", "contains".
- Keep texts short, and do not compress: one fact per sentence.
- Measurements and findings go in the PhD repository, not here.
- Branches are never named `claude/`. No co-author lines in commits or pull requests.
